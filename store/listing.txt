See every channel you follow that's live right now — with title, game, viewers, and uptime — right from your toolbar.

Key Features:
• Search by channel, title, or game
• Sort by viewers, broadcaster, game, or stream age — your choice is remembered
• Right-click any stream for quick links: channel, player, chat, clips, and more
• Optional raid-command copy button, Simple view, custom badge color, and more
• Live-count badge updates in the background, even with the popup closed

How to Use:
Log in with Twitch (read-only access to your follows) and you're set. No analytics, no tracking — everything stays in your browser.

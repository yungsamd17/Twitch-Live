Your ultimate companion for staying connected with your favorite Twitch streams.

Sam's Twitch Live surfaces the channels you follow that are currently live — searchable, sortable, and one click away from the stream, player, or chat.

FEATURES

• Live follow list: every followed channel that's live right now, with title, category, viewer count, and uptime.
• Quick search across channel name, title, and category.
• Sort by Broadcaster, Category, Viewers (high to low / low to high), Recently Started, or Longest Running — your choice is remembered between sessions.
• Right-click any stream to open its channel, player, or chat; visit its About, Videos, or Clips pages; or browse more streams in its category.
• Optional one-click button that copies the "/raid" command for a channel.
• Simple view (hides thumbnails and uptime), open-in-player, open-in-new-window, background refresh interval, and custom badge color.
• The toolbar badge counts your live channels on your schedule, even while the popup is closed. The open popup refreshes every 30 seconds.

HOW TO USE

1. Log in with your Twitch account (read-only access to the channels you follow — nothing is ever posted to your account).
2. Browse your live followed channels right from the toolbar popup.
3. Tune everything to your liking in Settings.

PRIVACY

Sign-in uses Twitch OAuth. Your access token and cached stream list are stored only in the extension's local browser storage. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no external server — the extension talks directly to the Twitch API. Logging out clears the token and cached data.
